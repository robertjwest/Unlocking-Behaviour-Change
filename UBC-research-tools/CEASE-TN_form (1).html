CEASE-TN Abstinence outcome reporting form

CEASE-TN — Consistent Evaluation of Abstinence: Standard for Endpoints (Tobacco / Nicotine)

A structured form for recording how an abstinence outcome is defined, measured, targeted and analysed — so that results from studies and services can be reported consistently and compared. Complete one form for each outcome assessment scenario (for example, a 4-week and a 26-week endpoint, or self-report and biochemically-verified versions), then export each as CSV or JSON — or build a set and export them together.

Suggested options appear as you type in most fields — but they are non-restrictive: any free text is accepted. Use “Not reported” where a source is silent and “Unclear” where it is ambiguous.
